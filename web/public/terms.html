Service Overview

Spam Trap is a free Discord moderation bot designed to detect spam, stop compromised accounts, and help keep Discord communities safe.

Eligibility

You must be at least 13 years old to use Spam Trap. You must also have permission from the server owner or administrators and grant the bot the required Discord permissions such as Ban Members and Manage Server.

Acceptable Use

Spam Trap must only be used for legitimate server moderation. You may not use the bot to harass users, target legitimate members, abuse its features, or violate Discord's Terms of Service.

How the Bot Works

Spam Trap automatically monitors configured trap channels. When spam is detected, it performs the configured moderation action, removes spam messages, notifies the affected user when appropriate, and records the action in your moderation logs.

Automatic Trap Channel

When Spam Trap joins a server, it automatically creates a spam trap channel with a multilingual warning message. Administrators can rename or replace this channel at any time.

Web Dashboard

The Spam Trap dashboard uses Discord OAuth2 for authentication. It only accesses your Discord username, avatar, and the list of servers you can manage. Login sessions automatically expire after seven days.
